# Design and product decisions

## Brief

Build a complete technology-community website and upload it under `alvarovillalbaa` on GitHub. The owner named the community **teki**, selected **Madrid** as its starting city, and chose a **GitHub community space for launch**. Spanish copy is an editorial choice for that first chapter.

## Sources inspected on 15 October 2026

- [mad.builders](https://mad.builders/): a concrete local home for builders, proof of work and repeatable encounters. Applied as a clear local identity and useful formats; no claims of a physical teki workspace.
- [Exponential](https://www.goexponential.org/): a community united by technical curiosity and ambition. Applied as a shared purpose; no fellowship, selection process, career-placement promise or affiliated partners.
- [Midnight Runners](https://www.midnightrunners.com/about-us): community and social connection as part of the activity. Applied as an approachable movement-and-coffee proposal.
- [Tracksmith](https://www.tracksmith.com/pages/about): craft, editorial identity and gathering around a common interest. Applied to typography, repeated rituals and an identity that can work on a club shirt.

These are inspirations, not partners. The typography, layouts, copy, diagrams and marks were authored for teki. No source-site assets or text were copied.

## Direction

Warm editorial identity: paper background, near-black type, cobalt blue, a lime invitation panel and an apricot build illustration. Oversized Bricolage Grotesque pairs with restrained italic Georgia. The asterisk suggests an intersection of people and ideas; the lowercase wordmark stays informal.

An asymmetrical hero pairs a direct promise with reference photography and a Madrid sticker. The page then moves through purpose, three concrete formats, community agreements, a GitHub invitation and practical questions. The three format panels are original HTML/CSS graphics and a licensed running photo. Motion is limited to short hover transitions with reduced-motion support.

## Function and content

- Three filters and an all-formats view, with pressed states and announced result counts.
- Native modal dialogs with focus restoration, Escape support, close button and backdrop dismissal.
- Mobile navigation with state synchronized to the viewport, Escape dismissal and anchor close.
- Native FAQ disclosures; static navigation, content and GitHub calls to action remain usable without JavaScript.
- All membership calls to action explain GitHub Discussions and its account requirement. No registration is simulated.
- Every format is visibly proposed. The site does not fabricate real-world events, dates, attendance, members or social proof.
- Self-hosted licensed imagery and font. No analytics, third-party embeds, browser storage or runtime API calls.

## Next operational input

The first real event requires an organizer, a date, a venue and confirmed conditions. Discuss those in the launch community. A custom domain and a future WhatsApp/Discord/Luma link can replace the default URLs without changing the architecture.
